# Mode: pronunciation — a sound or stress problem → 2 cards

The learner mispronounces a word: a sound (th in "think"), word stress ("comfortable"), a silent letter,
the number of syllables. Usually it comes from Eve's live correction or a talk review.

Examples:
- `/eve-add pron: think (I said "tink")`
- `/eve-add pron: comfortable — eu falo com-for-TA-ble`

## Cards — exactly 2, same `rule`

**Card 1 (recognise)**
- front: `🗣️ Say it out loud: ` + a short NEW sentence with the target word in `<b>`.
- back: the word with a simple respelling where CAPITALS mark the stressed syllable, plus IPA between
  slashes (e.g. `COMF-tə-bəl /ˈkʌmftəbəl/`), `<br>` one physical tip (where the tongue/lips go, which
  syllable is loud, how many syllables), and optionally `<br>⚠` the typical Brazilian slip
  (e.g. "tink" or "fink" for think; com-for-TA-ble).

**Card 2 (transfer)**
- front: `🗣️ Say it out loud: ` + a DIFFERENT word (in a short sentence, in `<b>`) with the same sound or
  stress pattern, so the learner learns the pattern, not one word. Or a minimal pair
  (`<b>think</b> · <b>sink</b> — make them different`) when the contrast is the point.
- back: respelling + IPA, `<br>` the shared rule in one line.

`rule` = `pron-<sound or word>` in kebab-case (`pron-th-voiceless`, `pron-stress-comfortable`,
`pron-silent-l`). Reuse an existing rule when the same problem comes back.
